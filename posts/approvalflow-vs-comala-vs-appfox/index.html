ApprovalFlow vs Comala vs AppFox: Confluence apps

Updated: 22 Oct, 2026
|

ApprovalFlow vs Comala vs AppFox is a choice about how your Confluence Cloud review process should work. Shortlist ApprovalFlow for version-aware review of published pages without hard content locks, Comala for configurable document lifecycle controls, and AppFox Workflows for visual workflow design and publishing controls. Then test the behavior your policy actually requires.

We make ApprovalFlow. This comparison describes documented capabilities, not a hands-on benchmark of the competing apps. Competitor Marketplace listings and linked vendor documentation were checked on October 21, 2026. We do not use install counts, old pricing snapshots, or a vendor’s choice of terminology to claim that another app lacks a feature.

Important: AppFox Workflows for Confluence is not AppFox Approvals for Confluence. They are separate products. The AppFox column below refers to Workflows.

Check native Confluence approvals first

Atlassian documents native approvals for pages on Confluence Premium and Enterprise. They support pages, not live docs or blog posts. Requesting or responding to an approval requires edit permission. Space admins can enable a requirement for approval before publishing new pages. Check availability and space settings on your own site before relying on these controls.

ApprovalFlow does not certify compliance or guarantee an audit outcome. Compare the controls and evidence your policy requires rather than assuming every approval tool enforces the same publishing rules.

Choose ApprovalFlow when the decision must identify the reviewed version

ApprovalFlow for Confluence is built on Atlassian Forge. It supports space-scoped workflows, submission from the page byline, reviewer decisions with comments, Approval Queue, and Workflow Analytics. Related page changes can be grouped into a Change Set with shared context and an optional reference link.

The practical boundary is just as important: ApprovalFlow reviews published pages and does not block publication. It does not lock content or replace Confluence permissions. Choose it when your process needs visible review decisions and version context while collaboration continues. If your requirement is that unapproved changes must be hidden from readers, you need a separate publishing or access control.

ApprovalFlow workflows are configured in the Confluence space. Start with the review stages your team needs rather than recreating an entire document lifecycle.

An edit does not automatically restart the workflow. The earlier decision retains its version context, stale detection exposes the mismatch, and the owner submits the current version when another review is required. See the version-aware review walkthrough and the Change Sets documentation for current-versus-approved version tracking.

Choose Comala when you need configurable document lifecycle controls

Comala Document Management’s Cloud listing describes lifecycle automation, role-based reviews, e-signatures, expirations, document activity, and workflow actions including restrictions and page copies. Its Cloud getting-started guide shows applying a workflow, assigning reviewers, deciding in the workflow-state dialog, and inspecting the document report.

That makes Comala worth evaluating when your requirements go beyond a review decision into managed lifecycle states and associated actions. Test the exact workflow, rather than assuming every template enforces the same controls. Cloud and Data Center documentation are separate; do not carry behavior from one edition into the other.

Choose AppFox Workflows when visual design and publishing controls matter

AppFox’s getting-started guide documents a drag-and-drop builder and templates for single-stage approval, multi-stage approval, content restrictions, and content expiration. The Workflows Marketplace listing also describes official major/minor versions, approval history, and publishing approved pages across spaces.

Evaluate it when owners need to design workflow logic and manage what readers see before and after approval. In the pilot, check source and destination space permissions, edited-page behavior, and which version reaches the publishing destination. A builder screenshot alone cannot establish those outcomes.

Test approval versions, audit evidence, and publication separately

Run the same small acceptance test in each app. Use a non-sensitive page and at least two accounts so that author and reviewer permissions are genuinely different.

Frequently asked questions

Which Confluence approval app should I choose?

Shortlist ApprovalFlow for soft, version-aware review of published pages; Comala for configurable document lifecycle controls; and AppFox Workflows for visual workflow design and publishing controls. Test your required behavior in the Cloud edition before choosing.

Are AppFox Workflows and Approvals the same app?

No. Workflows for Confluence and Approvals for Confluence are separate AppFox products. This comparison covers Workflows, which documents a drag-and-drop builder and multi-stage approvals. Do not apply a feature claim about one app to the other.

Does ApprovalFlow lock pages until approval?

No. ApprovalFlow reviews published pages and does not block publication, lock pages, or replace Confluence permissions. It records review decisions and page-version context while pages remain viewable according to existing permissions.

Does editing an approved page automatically start another review?

ApprovalFlow detects stale approval context, but an edit does not automatically restart the workflow. The owner needs to submit the current version for review when required. Test edit and resubmission behavior separately in every app you evaluate.

Which app is cheapest at my site size?

Use each app’s current Atlassian Marketplace pricing calculator with the same Confluence site size and billing term. This comparison does not rank prices from old per-user rates; licensing tiers and offers can change.
